What this investigation does—and does not—establish

Existing work already covers agent undo, selective merging and dependency-aware rollback. This prototype helps examine their interaction; it does not establish a new algorithm or a winning-worthy difference.

Supported: flat structured records, declared read dependencies, known writers, explicit preview, deterministic restoration. Not supported: arbitrary third-party apps, inferred causal relationships, unknown external writers, distributed transactions, sent messages or payments. A pause preserves the current record; it is not a completed recovery.
